Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Taskboard keeps your tasks on your own device. We don’t run a server that receives them, we don’t sell data, and there are no ads or analytics. If you choose to sign in with Google, your tasks are also saved to a private, hidden folder in your own Google Drive.

1. Who we are

Taskboard is a task, project and calendar planner operated by [Your name or company]. Questions about this policy can be sent to [your contact email].

2. What Taskboard stores

Everything you add to the app is stored in your browser’s local storage on your device:

  • Task names, descriptions, dates, repeat settings, reminder times and completion status
  • Project names
  • Photos you attach to tasks (resized and compressed in your browser before saving)
  • A small activity log and the record of which reminders have already fired

This data stays on your device unless you turn on Google sync (section 3). We never see it.

3. Optional Google sign-in and Drive sync

If you tap “Sign in with Google”, Taskboard asks for one permission: access to its own private app data folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope). Taskboard uses it to save and load a single file named taskboard.json so your tasks follow you across devices.

  • Taskboard cannot see, list or change any of your other Drive files.
  • Your tasks go directly between your browser and Google. They do not pass through any server of ours.
  • The Google access token is kept in memory only. Your browser remembers only that you signed in before, so it can reconnect without asking again.
  • Taskboard’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide sync, and is not used for advertising or shared with anyone.

4. Reminders and notifications

If you allow notifications, Taskboard uses your browser’s notification feature to show reminders. They are generated on your device. No push service or server is involved, and you can turn them off any time in your browser’s site settings.

5. Third-party services

When you open Taskboard, your browser loads resources from Google: the IBM Plex Sans font (Google Fonts) and Google’s sign-in library. Google receives standard request information such as your IP address and browser type, and may set its own cookies, as described in the Google Privacy Policy. Taskboard itself does not set cookies. Whoever hosts this site may also keep ordinary server access logs.

6. What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or share your data.
  • We don’t show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don’t create accounts or collect passwords. Sign-in is handled entirely by Google.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Local data: clear this site’s data in your browser settings to delete everything stored on the device. This also deletes tasks that have not been synced.
  • Sign out: choosing “Sign out” in Taskboard revokes its Google access token on that device. Your local tasks remain.
  • Drive data: to remove Taskboard’s access and its saved file, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions, open Taskboard, and delete its hidden app data.

8. Security

Data in browser storage is not encrypted, so anyone with access to your unlocked device and browser profile may be able to read it. Sync traffic with Google travels over HTTPS. Please avoid storing highly sensitive information such as passwords or financial details in tasks or photos.

9. Children

Taskboard is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. Material changes will be noted in the app or on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email [your contact email].
